On current plates the age identifier changes twice a year: in March it’s the year (26 = March 2026) and in September it’s the year plus 50 (76 = September 2026).

Private plates can move between vehicles, so a plate only tells you about the vehicle it was first issued to. A plate can never make a vehicle look newer than it is.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. Decoding is based on published DVLA rules and is a guide, not an official record.
